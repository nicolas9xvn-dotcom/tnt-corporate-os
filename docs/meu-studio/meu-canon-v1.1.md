## 1. IDENTITY

- Name: **MỀU** (Latin: MEU, Japanese: **メオ** — never メウ).
- A female calico cat character IP. Adult woman in personality (not a child, not a baby).
- Semi-anthropomorphic: walks on two legs, can hold objects, but her paws are **cat paws** (see §3.4).
- DNA feeling: **chảnh + sắc + nữ tính + đáng yêu** (sassy, sharp, feminine, cute).
- One sentence: *MỀU is a pretty, sassy, sharp-eyed calico cat whose flawless look contrasts with her everyday clumsy, over-confident, very human reactions.*

## 3. VISUAL DNA LOCKS

### 3.0 Body proportions (Proportion Master — locked 2026-09-24)
- Large round head: head including ears ≈ **2/5 of total height** (approximate, measured on the Proportion Master).
- **Slim, feminine body — NOT chibi, NOT chubby.** Narrow waist, slim arms.
- **Long slim legs** ≈ 1/3 of total height, cat feet; elegant standing with one leg crossing in front (model-like stance).
- Tail roughly as long as her body height, S-curve.
- If a generated MỀU looks short, stubby, toddler-like or baby-like → reject.

### 3.1 Fur & pattern
- Colors: white base, black/dark brown, warm soft orange.
- Head: white face and muzzle; black + orange calico patches on the top of the head and around both ears, as in Bộ 1. Do not move, add or remove patches.
- Orange patch on the back of one hind leg (as in Bộ 1).
- Soft, premium, detailed 3D fur.

### 3.2 Face
- Large round head, big amber/orange-brown eyes with thick upper lashes, defined dark eyebrows, small pink nose, small mouth, light pink cheeks.
- Signature: **side-eye (liếc xéo)** with one eyebrow raised.
- Eye color: amber/orange-brown as in Bộ 3 — do not drift to red or dark brown.

### 3.3 Tail — ONE TAIL ONLY
- Exactly one long, slim tail curving in an S-shape.
- Banding from tip: **white tip → black → orange → black …** as in Bộ 1.
- Never duplicate, split, or add a tail.

### 3.4 Paws — Sakura™ Paw Mark (Bộ 4)
- Real cat paws: **4 round pink toe pads + 1 normal pink main pad**.
- On the main pad: a **white Sakura-shaped birthmark**.
- The main pad itself is a normal cat pad — **never flower-shaped**.
- She can hold objects the way cartoon cats do (paw wraps around the object). Short, round, fluffy digits with tiny pink painted nails are allowed. **Never slender human fingers, never human hands.**
- The same paw design is used on every product (charm, sticker, logo, file, tag). Scale changes, proportions never change.

### 3.5 Signature accessories (Bộ 5)
- **MEU hair clip:** pink bow-shaped flower with gold rim and dots, pink sakura center, gold "MEU" lettering, gold rectangular clip. Worn on **her left ear** (viewer's right when she faces the camera).
- **MEU tail ring:** pink-red band with gold rims, gold "MEU" lettering, hanging pink sakura charm with gold center. Positioned on the lower-middle part of the tail.
- Never redesign, recolor, resize, or add accessories.

### 3.6 Phase 1 wardrobe lock — AME29 apron (Bộ 6)
- MỀU **always** wears the black quilted AME29 apron in every scene — at work, at home, eating, sleeping.
- Black quilted fabric, pink/red stitching, red ribbon tie at the waist side, front pocket with the pink Sakura™ paw emblem, small hanging tag.
- In-world reason (personality): she is so proud of her craft she never takes it off. This is a running joke.
- No other outfits in Phase 1.

### 3.7 Nail file (Bộ 7)
- Pink sanding file, rounded ends, raised 3D Sakura™ paw emblem at one end, "AME29 NAIL" wordmark on the left half.
- Colors: Sakura Pink #FF6FA8, White #FFFFFF, Dark Brown #5A262E.

### 3.9 Brand wording (locked 2026-09-24)
- Official wording on every MỀU asset: **AME29 NAIL** (not "NAIL OSAKA", not "NAIL SALON").
- Applies to apron (Bộ 6), nail file (Bộ 7), tags, packaging.
- Font/lockup: **[TBD]** — script "Ame29" (apron) vs sans "AME29" (file).

- Premium 3D animated film look, soft detailed fur, big glossy expressive eyes, warm pastel lighting, soft shadows.
- Avoid: cheap cartoon, flat 2D anime, photorealistic furry, overly childish chibi, generic AI fantasy.

## 4. PERSONALITY

**Outside:** elegant, confident, sharp, feminine, fashionable.
**Inside:** not perfect — a little clumsy, over-confident, quick to get annoyed, easily disappointed, over-reacts, loves food, loves sleep, a bit of drama, wants everything perfect but sometimes ruins it herself, tries to look cool but the situation betrays her.

**Comedy engine:** *Visual = elegant/confident* vs *Behavior = cute/chaotic/imperfect.*
Clumsiness is seasoning, not her identity — she is never a total klutz and she always recovers her dignity.

Favorite formulas:
- Situation → disaster → MỀU reaction
- MỀU wants something → over-confident → fails → keeps a smug face as if nothing happened

## 6. PHASE 1 FORMAT — "MỀU Daily"

- Length **10 s**, vertical **9:16**, one episode per day, produced in weekly batches of 7.
- Locations allowed: **(a) pink seamless studio background** like the Master images, with a few simple props; **(b) an AME29 nail table**.
  No house, no Osaka city, no husband in Phase 1.
- Structure: **0–2 s setup/hook → 2–7 s incident → 7–10 s reaction + punchline**. No intro. Music starts at frame 1; actions land on the beat.
- Camera: medium / medium-close; close-ups allowed for reactions and props. Keep her face readable.
- Default: **no dialogue**. Text overlays and logo are added in CapCut, never generated by AI.
- **Language: Japanese, Osaka dialect (大阪弁)** for every overlay and any spoken line. MỀU speaks casual Kansai: 〜や / 〜やで / 〜やん / 〜へん / 〜やねん / 〜やから. Short, sassy, tsukkomi-style (she comments on her own disasters as if they were nothing).
  Examples: 「三毛やで。」「問題あらへん。」「完璧やん。」「プロやからな。」
  Name: always メオ (never メウ).
  Every line must be checked by a native Kansai speaker before posting — badly imitated 大阪弁 reads as fake or mocking to Osaka viewers.
- Channel: **MỀU's own TikTok account** (separate from AME29). AME29 reposts/duets and links to it.
- Sound: playful beat, hard stops for comedic timing, small foley (clink, crinkle, "tsk").
